Floods start in many ways: heavy rain, storm surge, a river over its banks, a dead sump pump, a sewer backup or a major plumbing failure, and all of them call for fast professional help. Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. Day or night, 24/7, Boise Restoration Co sends flood cleanup crews throughout Boise to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

The first job is getting the water out quickly, and our submersible and truck-mounted pumps are able to clear thousands of gallons every hour. With the standing water gone, our IICRC-certified technicians check the contamination level and apply the right sanitizing steps, including antimicrobial treatment, HEPA air filtration and disinfection of every affected surface. Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

Then the drying begins: high-velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers of the LGR (low-grain refrigerant) type go in to pull moisture out of the building's structural parts. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. From start to finish we document each step for your insurance claim, with the moisture maps, equipment logs, photos and scope-of-work reports adjusters ask for.

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

When to Call for Flood Cleanup in Boise

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source
  • Storm damage that lets rain in through the roof or walls
  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • Appliances such as a water heater, washer or dishwasher spilling water across the floor
  • Water coming in through foundation cracks, window wells or door thresholds
  • Flooring that squelches when you walk on it and walls damp to the touch
  • Receding water that leaves muddy residue and debris behind

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls
  • Roof runoff should leave the downspout far enough out that it cannot soak the foundation
  • Use waterproof bins and raised shelving for anything valuable kept at ground level or below
  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property
  • Make sure the ground slopes away from your foundation on every side
  • Look into a flood insurance policy, including when your home sits outside a mapped flood zone
